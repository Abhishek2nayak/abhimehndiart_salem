Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book bridal mehndi?
Bridal dates fill quickly, especially in wedding season (Oct–Feb). We recommend booking 3–6 months in advance for bridal, and at least 2 weeks for engagement, party or festival bookings.
Do you provide home service in Salem?
Yes — home service is available across Salem with no travel charges within the city. We travel to Coimbatore, Trichy, Bengaluru and beyond for bridal bookings; travel fee depends on distance.
Is the henna 100% natural?
Absolutely. We mix our own paste fresh from pure organic henna powder with essential oils — no PPD, no chemical dyes, no shortcuts. Safe for sensitive skin and gives the deepest, longest-lasting stain.
How long does a bridal session take?
Full bridal (both hands + both feet with fine detail) typically runs 4–8 hours depending on coverage and complexity. We plan the session around your muhurtham with buffer time.
What are your rates?
Every design is quoted individually based on coverage, detail and travel. Message us your event, date and reference photos on WhatsApp for a quick, honest quote — group and off-season discounts available.
How do I book?
Fastest way is WhatsApp at +91 87996 68439, or use the contact form and we'll get back within a day.
