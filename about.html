Meet Abhi

A cone, a customer, and complete focus

Abhi began applying mehndi for family weddings as a teenager in Salem, drawing on the same florals and temple borders her grandmother once designed for local brides. What started as helping relatives before big functions grew into a full-time craft — today Abhi works with brides, families and event planners across Salem and neighbouring towns for every kind of celebration, from a quiet valaikaappu at home to a three-day wedding with a hundred guests waiting their turn.

Every appointment starts with a short conversation: your outfit, your event, how bold or delicate you like your hands to look. The goal is always the same — a design that photographs beautifully and still feels like you.

The journey

From family functions to full-time studio

A short timeline of how Abhi Mehndi Art came to be.

The early years

First cone, first cousin's wedding

Learned traditional Kongu-region florals from family, practising on paper before ever touching skin.

Building a name

Word of mouth across Salem

Bridal bookings grew through referrals — one happy bride leading to her cousins, friends and colleagues.

Going full-time

Abhi Mehndi Art opens its doors

Added Arabic and fusion styles alongside traditional designs, and began taking bookings for engagements, baby showers and festivals.

Today

A full wedding-season calendar

Now trusted by families across Salem for every occasion that calls for henna, big or small.
