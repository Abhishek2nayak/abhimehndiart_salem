Who we are

Beautiful mehndi, drawn with heart

Abhi Mehndi Art is a renowned mehndi service known for beautiful and intricate designs across India. With over a decade of experience and a team of skilled mehndi artists, we specialize in bridal, traditional, Arabic, festival, and engagement mehndi for every special occasion.

Using 100% organic, chemical-free mehndi, our artists create unique designs that blend traditional artistry with the latest trends. With a strong focus on quality, creativity, professionalism, and customer satisfaction, Abhi Mehndi Art continues to build trust and expand its services across different locations in India.
